This Privacy Policy explains how Siren Hero Inc. collects and uses contact information in connection with its operational safety and dispatch services.

Contact information we collect

We may collect contact information provided by authorized event medical and operations personnel, including names, email addresses, and mobile phone numbers. We may also maintain records of SMS opt-in, consent, and opt-out requests.

We use contact information to provide and support Siren Hero services. Mobile phone numbers may be used to send operational safety and dispatch-related SMS notifications, including notifications associated with event activity.

No marketing sharing

Mobile phone numbers and SMS opt-in or consent information are not sold, rented, shared, or provided to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

SMS notices and choices

Message frequency varies based on event activity. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to opt out of future SMS messages. Reply HELP for help and support information.

Contact us

For privacy questions or SMS support, contact support@sirenhero.co.
