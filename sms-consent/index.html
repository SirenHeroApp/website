SMS Consent

This page documents the verbal SMS consent process used by Siren Hero Inc. for authorized event medical and operations personnel. SMS consent is not collected through this website.

Verbal opt-in during onboarding

During onboarding, authorized event medical and operations personnel may verbally opt in to receive low-volume operational safety and dispatch SMS notifications from Siren Hero.

Verbal consent script

“Would you like to receive Siren Hero operational safety and dispatch alerts by SMS? Message frequency varies based on event activity. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time and HELP for help. SMS enrollment is optional.”

Enrollment requirements

The staff member must affirmatively agree before their mobile number is enrolled. Declining SMS does not prevent participation; other dispatch methods may be used.

Messages are operational and transactional only, not marketing.

Support and policies

For SMS support, contact support@sirenhero.co.
